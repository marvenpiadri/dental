# ToothFairy3 meshes

Surfaces of the 32 teeth and their pulps from case ToothFairy3F_026, made from the CBCT labels
(marching cubes, Taubin smoothing, simplification) by the cd.dali project.

Bolelli F, Lumetti L, Vinayahalingam S and colleagues. ToothFairy3, MICCAI 2025.
https://ditto.ing.unimore.it/toothfairy3/ - licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
These meshes are derivatives and carry the same licence. One patient, 0.3 mm voxels: a teaching shape, not a norm.

teeth.bin / teeth.json are derived from those surfaces by tools/tooth-lib/build.py (cut at the neck, offset shells, baked
surface weights) and therefore carry the same licence, CC BY-SA 4.0. The hardware parts (implant, abutment, bracket, band)
are generated by the same script and are original.
